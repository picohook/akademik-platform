# LibRead demo — sources and licences

The demo shows two public-domain NASA books. NASA is not a partner of this demo and does not endorse it.

## Earth at Night
NASA Science Mission Directorate. (2019). *Earth at Night*. NASA. https://www.nasa.gov/ebooks/earth-at-night/

Five illustrated chapters and the two source sections (acknowledgments, cover image credits) were selected from the original EPUB; text, images and alt texts are the original. The acknowledgments state that content produced with NASA funding is in the public domain and may be used freely. Cover credit: NASA's Earth Observatory / NOAA / DOD.

## Earth as Art
Friedl, L., & Yuen, K. (2012). *Earth as Art*. NASA. https://www.nasa.gov/ebooks/earth-as-art/

Pages 12–22 of the 172-page PDF, unchanged (real pages with their text). Image credits printed in the book (NASA, USGS and others) apply.

## What the demo does with your data
Notes, favourites and reading progress are kept in this browser only. Nothing is sent to LibRead or LibEdge, and no account is used. "Reset demo" removes them.
